Ω's Examination

Prove your worth to join OMEGA

Everything related to the test will be completed within this server, no exceptions. The test must be done 1-3 all in order.

Act as a sensible human being and behave appropriately.

1 test-1 Fruit

Find the specified fruit (the @Eater who is online at the time will decide which fruit to look for) and send a screenshot or video of the fruit in a form that proves it is you. (You can upload up to 50MB to the server. You can also upload the video to youtube and send a link to it ).

If you try to pass the exam by illegal means, you will be given the @Brainrot role and banned from taking the exam for one month. In such a case, the test must be taken again from the beginning.

2 test-2 Speech

・How long you have been doing narrow.one

・K/D of your personal account (If you usually use a clan account, describe how long you have been using that account and how much coin you have accumulated so far)

・The clan you belong to

・Your Strengths What you can do, such as video editing or mod development (it doesn't have to be only related to narrow.one, what you will contribute by joining Ω)

＊If you are found to have used a generated AI (chat.GPT, deepseek, Gemini AI, etc.) in your self-introduction speech, you will be given the @Brainrot role and banned from taking exams for one month. In that case, the test must be taken again from the beginning.

3 test-3 PVP

1v1 with @Trainer 10 points match by anybow. If you can get 8 points or more, you will advance to the vote. No deuces,but death by fall or other non-kill factors will count. Worlds will be decided by @Trainer , no specifics, but exams are not allowed on foggy worlds. If you unfortunately fail the test, you will be given the @Challenger role and can take the PVP test again after 24 hours.

★ Final test vote

If the vote is approved by a majority of all members, the member may become a member. However, any member who has not responded to the vote shall be deemed to have voted no.

The results of the vote will be communicated as soon as they are finalized, but the only thing that will be communicated is whether the test was passed or not.

If a member unfortunately fails the exam, he/she may take the exam again from the beginning after 7 days.

＊If you are seen contributing to Ω by boosting the server, actively participating in events, etc., you may receive an advantage in the vote.

＊If a member commits multiple acts of cheating or lacks common sense, he/she may be given a @Brainlot role, which will permanently ban him/her from taking the exam. If you want it lifted, send me a DM including the reason why.
