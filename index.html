OMEGA's History

Omega was originally Pekora's personal account. However, before May, three strong Japan people were called up and a clan called PST was created. In addition, the origin of PST is an acronym for the members Pekora, Shoyu, and Tokisudeniosushi. Later, when Tokisudeniosushi retired, Kenshin was called up to fill the void. Due to this, the name of the clan was changed to PSK. At the beginning of August, PSK convened a meeting of members consisting only of Japan. At this time, the name of the clan was changed to Omega. At the beginning of September, we merged with a clan called GG, but we canceled the merger contract because there were many cases of unsolicited purchases even when we warned them. Later, stun removed the Omega share, but with the help of BigJoe and Jesper, we were able to successfully reinstate the account. Then, in November, when the dust was over, OMEGA allowed foreigners to participate. Omega is made up of the current members. Thanks guys. And if you become a member, you will make OMEGA history.
